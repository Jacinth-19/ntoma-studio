privacy_title = Privacy
privacy_intro = Short version: Ntoma works on your phone. We only send an image somewhere when you ask for AI generation, and we say so when it happens.
privacy_images_title = What images are processed
privacy_images_body = Fabric photos you capture or pick, and — only if you use try-on — one photo of a person. Generated looks are stored in the app's private storage.
privacy_device_title = Do images leave my device?
privacy_device_body = On-device analysis: no. Your fabric photo is analysed on the phone and never uploaded. Cloud analysis and AI try-on: yes — the image is sent to the configured backend so the model can process it. The engine in use is always shown on the analysis screen.
privacy_retention_title = How long are images kept?
privacy_retention_body = Locally, until you delete them. A connected backend decides its own retention; we recommend a maximum of 24 hours for uploaded images, and the demo engine keeps nothing.
privacy_delete_title = How do I delete my data?
privacy_delete_body = Use Delete my data below. It removes scans, analyses, looks, history, favourites and cached images from this device immediately.
privacy_analytics_title = Analytics
privacy_analytics_body = This build ships with a debug-only logger. No analytics SDK is included and no usage data leaves your device.
privacy_ads_title = Advertising
