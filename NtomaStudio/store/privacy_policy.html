Ntoma Studio — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 September 2026

Ntoma Studio ("the app") is designed around a simple principle: your photos and choices stay on your device. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not collect.

What we collect

Nothing is transmitted by this build. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting transmission, no advertising SDK and no advertising identifier. There is no account, no sign-in and no server-side profile.

What stays on your device

DataWhere it livesPurpose
Fabric & person photos you capture or pick App-private storageAnalysis, try-on previews, your wardrobe and favourites
Analysis history, favourites, collections, measurements On-device databaseApp features only
Display name & preferences On-device preferences storeGreeting, theme, engine choice
Crash traces (if a crash occurs) On-device log folder, max 10 filesDebugging on your device; never uploaded
Contributed training photos (optional) On-device until you export themOnly if you explicitly consent and tap Export; the resulting zip is shared through Android's share sheet, i.e. you choose the destination

Permissions

PermissionWhy
CameraCapturing fabric and person photos, after an in-app explanation. The app never records audio or video.
Notifications (optional)Processing and recommendation notices on newer Android versions; you can decline.

The app uses the system Photo Picker to let you choose images, so it never needs broad storage access. It does not request contacts, location, microphone, SMS, phone, call-log, accessibility or Bluetooth permissions.

On-device AI

Fabric analysis and try-on previews run on your device with bundled models and are labelled honestly in the app (e.g. "on-device demo"). If a future version offers an optional cloud analysis engine, photos would be sent only to a server URL that you enter yourself in Settings, and this policy and the in-app notices would be updated to say so before that feature ships.

Your choices & deletion

Settings → Data controls → Delete my data removes the on-device database, media files and usage quotas in one tap. Uninstalling the app removes everything else, since nothing lives outside your device.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above (for example, adding an optional cloud feature), this policy will be updated in-app and on the store listing before the change takes effect.
